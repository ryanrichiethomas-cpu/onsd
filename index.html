How a night works

Five minutes a round, until everyone has met.

Men and women face each other at long tables. Every conversation lasts 5 minutes. Then the bell.

  1. 01

    Take your seat

    Each night is for a set age range. You sit across from someone at a long table and talk for 5 minutes.

  2. 02

    The bell rings

    The men switch seats and it starts again, round after round, until everyone has met. Expect to meet 7 to 15 people.

  3. 03

    Mark your card

    For each person you meet, mark your match card: "friend" or "more".

  4. 04

    Get your matches

    Scores are sent 1 to 72 hours after the last round. Mutual matches receive each other's contact info.

Good to know

Plain answers, no surprises.

The short version of the policies. Buying a ticket means accepting Ontario Speed Dating's purchase agreement.

Is a match guaranteed?

No. Nobody can promise a match, and we do not. There is also no guarantee of a preferred free drink, a prize, or that every registered person will arrive. The one guaranteed item is a free item for on time arrivals at running events, unless the event description says otherwise.

What happens if an event is canceled?

You choose: a refund to your original payment method, or a free transfer to another event.

Can I transfer my ticket?

Yes, free of charge. Get in touch before the night's sales close with the new person's name and email. If you move to a cheaper night, the difference is refunded.

Can I cancel my own ticket?

Refund requests for regular events must arrive no later than 96 hours before sales close, and a non-refundable service fee is deducted. Special events are not refunded unless the event is canceled.

How do I reach support?

By email, and in person by appointment. Support is not available by phone. Replies take 2 to 48 hours. Use the contact form below to write to us.

Which payments are accepted?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, JCB, UnionPay, Visa Debit, Mastercard Debit, Google Pay, Apple Pay, Interac E-transfer, and cash by appointment. Card payments are processed by Clover. PayPal is not accepted.

Where can I buy tickets?

Only on this site. Tickets bought from third parties, including Eventbrite and Ticketmaster, are not accepted for entry.

Do I need ID?

Bring photo ID in case your age is disputed: a Canadian passport, an Ontario Photo Card or an Ontario Driver's Licence. Health cards are not accepted. Pub crawls need government photo ID for the clubs.

How do I know an email is really from you?

Every email from Ontario Speed Dating is sent from the ontariospeeddating.ca domain.
